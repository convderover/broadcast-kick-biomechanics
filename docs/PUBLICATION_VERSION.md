The laboratory KLI–ankle-speed correlation (Spearman rho = 0.79) is a **within-model consistency observation with shared kinematic input**. Broadcast KLI agreement is poor: ICC = 0.090 (95% CI 0.018–0.181), r = 0.204, calibration slope b = 0.081. The posture surrogate gives r = 0.303, ICC = 0.275, b = 0.380; its video-transfer model was fitted to all MoVi laboratory observations and was not a held-out participant evaluation. **Calibration slopes are not reliability estimates.** The previous approximately 590-injury calculation and league-season forecasts are withdrawn. For illustrative HR = 1.5, independent assumed information fractions q = 1, 0.5, 0.2 and 0.1 give 48, 96, 239 and 478 rounded-up hypothetical events. These are not sample-size estimates for KLI.

La correlación KLI–velocidad del tobillo (rho = 0,79) muestra **consistencia del modelo con entrada cinemática compartida**. El acuerdo del KLI desde vídeo es pobre: CCI = 0,090 (IC 95% 0,018–0,181), r = 0,204, b = 0,081. El sustituto postural obtiene r = 0,303, CCI = 0,275 y b = 0,380, con transferencia ajustada usando todos los participantes. **Las pendientes no estiman fiabilidad.** Se retiran el cálculo de unas 590 lesiones y las predicciones de temporadas. Los escenarios HR = 1,5 y q independiente = 1, 0,5, 0,2 y 0,1 dan 48, 96, 239 y 478 eventos hipotéticos, no tamaños muestrales para validar este KLI.

The main benchmark conditions use reference-derived scale and alignment and known kicking leg and contact. The selected criteria are exploratory, not validated football decision thresholds. Longitudinal monitoring, clinical utility, scouting decisions and injury prediction were not validated. The field example comprises 13 kicks by the author without a reference measurement. Reference data comprise 7 CMU ball kicks from 2 people and 83 MoVi air kicks; usable real video comprises 162 recordings from 82 MoVi participants; detector residuals come from 200 3DSP shots.

Las condiciones principales usan escala y alineamiento de referencia y pierna y contacto conocidos. Los criterios son exploratorios; no se ha validado seguimiento longitudinal ni utilidad clínica, de selección o predictiva. El campo son 13 golpeos del autor sin referencia. CMU aporta 7 golpeos de 2 personas; MoVi, 83 golpeos al aire; el vídeo real utilizable son 162 grabaciones de 82 personas; los residuos 2D proceden de 200 disparos de 3DSP.
